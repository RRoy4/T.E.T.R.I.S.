### Week 1 Targets

### 1\. ROS Learning

* Everyone gets familiar with ROS before simulation work begins.

* Resources: SOR, official ROS documentation, MoGiROS and YouTube tutorials.

* Focus on basic concepts, architecture, nodes, topics and workflows.

### 2\. Component Selection

* Research and shortlist components based on:

    * Cost, weight and dimensions.

    * Availability and procurement feasibility.

    * Compatibility with the overall design.

    * Availability of CAD models and datasheets.

* Prefer components with readily available CAD models to speed up CAD assembly.

### 3\. Docking & Undocking Mechanism

* Explore existing docking and undocking mechanisms.

* Review relevant research papers and existing designs.

* Decide whether to adapt an existing mechanism or develop our own based on feasibility and requirements.

### 4\. Documentation & Git

* Document design decisions, component choices, research, simulation setups and progress.

* Record rejected ideas and the reasoning behind them.

* Keep the Git repository and relevant branches updated and organized. along with their reasoning and keep your git branch updated with your resources
